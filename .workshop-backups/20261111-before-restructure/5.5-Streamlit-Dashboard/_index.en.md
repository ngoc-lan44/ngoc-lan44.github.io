### Procedure

Verify the dashboard service/health and Athena access, then inspect four overview and four weekly KPIs. Charts cover posting date, skills, location, companies, experience, level and salary disclosure. Weekly comparisons use retained listings, not complete market history.

Test keyword/location/level/experience/company/salary filters, empty data and reset. Select job details and compare job_id/title/company/ITviec URL. Do not treat a healthy endpoint as proof of successful Athena queries or a single smoke run as a load test.

### Result and images

Capture eight KPIs with date range, actual Plotly charts, filters and matching details. The November 2 acceptance result remains historical.
